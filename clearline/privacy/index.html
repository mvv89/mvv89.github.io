Clearline Privacy Policy

Effective: October 30, 2026 · Developer: MVV Apps · Contact: mvvapps.dev@gmail.com

Clearline blocks adult websites and searches on your Android phone and helps you keep a clean streak. It works on the device and has no accounts, analytics or ads.

Accessibility Service

Clearline uses the Android AccessibilityService API, which you turn on yourself after an in-app explanation. The service is limited to the browsers installed on your phone and to Reddit, X, Telegram and Tumblr. In browsers it reads only the text of the address bar and checks it against a list of adult domains and words stored on your phone. In the other apps it only notices that the app is open, if you chose to block it.

  • It does not read web pages, messages or other text you see or type.
  • It does not keep a history of the sites you visit.
  • None of this data leaves your phone.

Data stored on your phone

Your settings, the sites and words you add, daily counts of blocks, your streak, your reasons to quit and the slips you choose to record. A partner lock is stored only as a one-way hash of the code. All of it stays on the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Private DNS

If you follow the in-app guide, your phone sends DNS lookups to the family filter you set up (CleanBrowsing by default), as with any Private DNS provider. Clearline itself only reads which Private DNS hostname is configured, to show whether the filter is on. See CleanBrowsing's privacy policy.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play. Our purchase service, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts so that Pro can be unlocked and restored. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

App updates

Clearline downloads fixes from its update server, operated by Expo. The request contains the app version and a random installation ID, and nothing about you or your browsing. See Expo's privacy policy.

Diagnostics (optional)

Off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, Clearline records identifiers of screen elements in supported apps (never text or web addresses) on your phone. They are sent only if you tap "Share diagnostics" and choose where to send them, and are used only to support more browsers.

Sharing, age, retention

We do not sell or share personal data. Clearline is intended for adults (18+). To ask about or delete data linked to your purchases, email us at the address above.
